# Typographic Poster

Use when brand copy is strong and imagery would distract.

- Concept job: make words, rhythm, scale, and contrast carry the identity.
- Structure: large editable headline, secondary editable phrase, geometric framing, one accent mark.
- Good assets: font choices, editable text, shape rules, subtle gradient or lighting.
- Avoid: baked text in SVG/image, decorative font chaos, too many styles.
- Validation: all important typography is editable; text bounds fit safe zone; font choices are recorded.
