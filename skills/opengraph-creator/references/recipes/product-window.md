# Product Window

Use when the app UI, screenshot, dashboard, editor, or product surface is the strongest proof.

- Concept job: show the product as a crafted object without becoming a screenshot dump.
- Structure: one product window or device-like frame, route-specific callout, brand accent, editable title/subtitle.
- Good assets: repo screenshot, app icon, UI chrome shapes, editable labels, subtle depth shadows.
- Avoid: unreadable full-page screenshots, fake metrics, too many panels.
- Validation: screenshot is a packaged asset; text overlays remain editable; crop/focal point fits the 1200x630 card.
