# Data Signal

Use for analytics, finance, reports, monitoring, search, AI tooling, news intelligence, or signal-heavy apps.

- Concept job: make information feel discoverable, precise, and alive.
- Structure: signal path, chart-like motif, timeline, radar, or data field paired with clear editable copy.
- Good assets: SVG/D3 paths, route-specific metrics from repo copy, small icon marks, controlled glow.
- Avoid: meaningless graphs, fake numbers unless marked decorative, random node clouds.
- Validation: data marks are concept-specific; no unreadable microtext; text remains editable.
