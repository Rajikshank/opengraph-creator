# Document Stack

Use for docs, blogs, reports, newsletters, legal, education, or workflow apps.

- Concept job: communicate layered knowledge or a polished deliverable.
- Structure: stacked sheets/cards, clipped screenshot or page excerpt, editable title and badge.
- Good assets: shape layers, repo screenshots, SVG page lines, brand stamp, soft shadows.
- Avoid: boring blank cards, fake paragraphs as important text, cluttered paper piles.
- Validation: document shapes are editable; any placeholder lines are decorative; real title/subtitle remain text layers.
